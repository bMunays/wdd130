Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe is a southern African nation defined by its rich heritage, dramatically anchored by the majestic stone architecture of the ancient Great Zimbabwe ruins in Masvingo. From the thundering cascade of Victoria Falls to its vast, wildlife-rich savannas, the country blends breathtaking natural wonders with profound historical depth. Guided by the resilient spirit of its people, it stands as a land of enduring cultural pride and captivating landscapes.
