Half Day Adventure

Perfect for families and beginners! Enjoy a scenic 3 to 4-hour journey down calm Class II and III rapids. Take in the beautiful canyon views and experience the thrill of the river in a relaxed, safe environment.

Big Mallard

An exhilarating full-day trip designed for those seeking a bit more excitement. Navigate through continuous Class III and IV whitewater. Lunch is provided on a pristine sandy beach halfway down the river.

The The Devil's Teeth

Our premier multi-day expedition for experienced thrill-seekers. Conquer massive Class IV and V rapids over two unforgettable days. Includes overnight camping, fireside meals, and spectacular star-gazing.
